# Annotationes v1.0.0: The Spiritual Exercises in the Twentieth Century

First archived release of **Annotationes**, a reception apparatus for the *Spiritual Exercises* of Ignatius of Loyola in the twentieth century and the continuation of [Ignatiana](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22682750). Live site: https://annotationes.netlify.app/

## What it is

The text stands at the centre on the paragraph grid [1]–[370]: the Spanish Autograph and the Latin Vulgate after the Monumenta Ignatiana (Madrid 1919), Elder Mullan's English (1914), Joseph Rickaby's English (2nd ed. 1923) and Alfred Feder's German (1922). The century's readings stand in the margin, each where it touches the text.

- **757 glosses** from 32 works and 104 journal articles in six languages (English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Latin).
- **Commentaries:** Longridge 1919 and Rickaby 1923 in full; Feder 1922 with his 108 endnotes; Przywara's *Deus semper maior* (1938–40) anchored page by page, with his own index of the numbers of the Exercises transcribed by hand; Torti 1909.
- **Readers outside the commentaries:** Joyce's retreat sermons (1916), 28 passages of Jung's *Collected Works*, the papal documents of 1922 and 1929, Marcos 1923.
- **Spanish and Italian before 1931:** eleven articles of *Razón y Fe* (1904–1919) and Codina's *Los orígenes de los Ejercicios* (1926).
- **Journals:** the *Zeitschrift für Aszese und Mystik*, *Geist und Leben*, *Studies in the Spirituality of Jesuits*, *The Way*, the *Revue d'ascétique et de mystique*; twelve articles read in full and summarized.

Every gloss states how it was placed (passage read, table of contents, the book's own index, page, title) and its access tier: public-domain texts are quoted and linked to their scans, freely available ones are linked, protected works are represented by anchors and summaries only.

## Ways in

- **Reader** with margin, density strip and filters by language, access, genre and decade.
- **Eight reading paths**, among them *Where does the book come from? The sources debate, 1909–1956* and *The King and his following, 1929–1942*.
- **Introduction** essay, **timeline** of the works in five lines (1901–1978), **concept atlas** of 68 concepts joined by co-occurrence, **bibliography** of the whole apparatus in APA style with BibTeX and RIS export, and public-domain **plates**.
